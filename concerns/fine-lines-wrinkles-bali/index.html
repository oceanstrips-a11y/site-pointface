Anti-Aging & Firming Facials in Uluwatu, Bali: Fine Lines, Wrinkles & Sagging

Prevent the signs of aging, boost collagen and keep the skin from sagging, without needles. Our anti-aging facials combine Korean peptides, radiofrequency, microcurrent and red LED to firm the skin and soften the look of lines.

Book a skin analysis Recommended treatments

What is it?

Fine lines are shallow creases that appear first around the eyes, mouth and forehead. Over time, as collagen and elastin decline and the skin loses water, they can deepen into wrinkles. The good news: early lines respond very well to hydration, collagen-supporting care and consistent sun protection.

What causes it?

  • Natural decline in collagen and elastin from our mid-twenties, which leads to loss of firmness and sagging
  • UV exposure — the main driver of premature skin aging (photoaging)
  • Dehydration, which makes lines look deeper
  • Repeated expressions, squinting in bright light and jaw clenching
  • Stress, poor sleep and long-haul travel

Why Bali makes it worse

Bali sits at 8° south of the equator: the UV index is high all year round, and long days on the beach, the surf and the clifftop make cumulative exposure add up fast. Salt water, wind and air-conditioning dry the surface of the skin, so lines look more visible — even on younger skin.

Our approach at POINT · FACE

We start with a complimentary skin analysis to see where lines come from — dehydration, loss of firmness or muscle tension. We then combine three levers: deep hydration (hyaluronic acid, snail mucin), collagen support (peptides, red LED, radiofrequency, microcurrent) and muscle work (Kobido and Buccal massage) for a natural lift.

FAQ

Anti-Aging & Firming: your questions

Can a facial really reduce wrinkles?

Facials cannot erase deep wrinkles, but they can soften the look of fine lines, improve hydration and support firmness over a series of sessions. For deep static wrinkles, a dermatologist can advise on medical options.

At what age should I start anti-aging facials?

Prevention works best from your late twenties. Before that, focus on hydration and sun protection — our Ultimate Glass Skin facial is a great start.

Are your anti-aging facials safe during pregnancy?

Radiofrequency and microcurrent are not recommended during pregnancy. Tell us when booking and we will suggest a pregnancy-friendly option such as the Hydrating Ritual.
