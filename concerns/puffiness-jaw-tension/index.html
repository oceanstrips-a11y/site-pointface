Face Sculpting Massage in Uluwatu — Puffiness & Jaw Tension

A clenched jaw, a puffy morning face, features that look tired. Our sculpting massages work the muscles and lymph for a defined, relaxed face — naturally.

Book a skin analysis Recommended treatments

What is it?

Facial puffiness comes from fluid retention and sluggish lymphatic drainage. Jaw tension comes from overworked muscles — especially the masseter — often from stress, clenching or grinding at night.

What causes it?

  • Fluid retention from heat, salt, alcohol and flights
  • Poor lymphatic drainage and lack of sleep
  • Stress-related jaw clenching and teeth grinding
  • Tight facial and neck muscles

Why Bali makes it worse

Heat, long flights and holiday dinners make the face puff up; stress you bring from home often lives in the jaw. A sculpting massage is one of the fastest ways to see — and feel — the difference.

Our approach at POINT · FACE

We combine lymphatic drainage to depuff, deep-tissue work to release the muscles (including from inside the mouth with Buccal massage) and lifting techniques to redefine contours.

Daily care in Bali

FAQ

Puffiness & Jaw Tension: your questions

What is buccal massage?

An intra-oral facial massage: with gloved hands, your therapist works the cheek and jaw muscles from inside the mouth as well as outside, reaching muscles a normal face massage cannot.

Kobido, Buccal or Gua Sha?

Buccal for deep jaw tension and contour, Kobido for a lifting effect, Gua Sha for relaxation and gentle drainage. They combine beautifully.
