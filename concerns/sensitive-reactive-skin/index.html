Facials for Sensitive & Reactive Skin in Uluwatu, Bali

Redness, stinging, heat, reactions to new products. Sensitive skin needs facials that calm and repair — never strip.

Book a skin analysis Recommended treatments

What is it?

Sensitive skin reacts easily — with redness, heat, tightness or itching — usually because its barrier is weakened and lets irritants in more easily.

What causes it?

  • A compromised skin barrier
  • Over-exfoliation or too many active ingredients
  • Sun, heat and sudden temperature changes
  • Fragrance or alcohol in products
  • Genetic tendency (e.g. rosacea-prone skin)

Why Bali makes it worse

Heat, sun and switching between outdoor humidity and cold AC can trigger flushing. New products bought on holiday and more frequent cleansing after the beach can tip sensitive skin over the edge.

Our approach at POINT · FACE

Low-intensity, barrier-first: gentle hydrodermabrasion, soothing centella, PDRN and peptides, cooling cryo massage and green or yellow LED. Every step is adapted on the day after your skin analysis.

FAQ

Sensitive & Reactive Skin: your questions

Is Hydrafacial OK for sensitive skin?

Yes, when adapted: we reduce suction and acid strength and focus on soothing actives. Tell us about any reactions or prescriptions during the skin analysis.

Can you treat rosacea?

We can calm and hydrate rosacea-prone skin, but rosacea is a medical condition — please follow your dermatologist's advice and tell us about any treatment you use.
