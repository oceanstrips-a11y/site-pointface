After-Sun & Post-Surf Facials in Uluwatu, Bali

Surf sessions at Bingin, afternoons at Padang Padang, sunsets on the cliffs. Our after-sun facials cool, calm and repair skin that has seen a lot of sun.

Book a skin analysis Recommended treatments

What is it?

Sun stress shows up as heat, redness, tightness, peeling and, over time, uneven tone and premature lines. Even without a visible burn, UV exposure weakens the skin barrier and triggers inflammation.

What causes it?

  • Intense UV — high all year round near the equator
  • Reflection from water and sand
  • Sunscreen washed off by surfing and swimming
  • Salt water and wind drying the skin
  • Heat that keeps the skin inflamed

Why Bali makes it worse

Uluwatu is one of the world's great surf destinations — which means hours in the water with sunscreen slowly washing off and sunlight bouncing off the ocean. Many of our Bingin clients come straight from the beach.

Our approach at POINT · FACE

First we cool and calm (cryo globe, aloe, centella, glacial clay, green & yellow LED), then we repair (PDRN, peptides, hyaluronic acid) and finally we protect. We always adapt intensity: no strong exfoliation on freshly sun-stressed skin.

At home

Daily care in Bali

  • Reapply a water-resistant SPF 50 every 2 hours and after surfing
  • Use a zinc stick on the nose and cheekbones in the water
  • Rinse salt water off and apply aloe or a soothing gel
  • Avoid acids and retinol while the skin is red

FAQ

Sun-Stressed Skin: your questions

Can I get a facial with a sunburn?

For mild redness and heat, yes — the Cryo Skin Reset is designed for it. For blistering or peeling burns, wait until the skin has healed.

Can I surf after my facial?

We recommend waiting 24 hours before surfing or long sun exposure, and always applying SPF 50.
