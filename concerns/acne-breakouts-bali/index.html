Acne, Breakouts & Blackheads Facials in Uluwatu, Bali

Humidity, sweat, sunscreen and salt water — Bali is tough on congested skin. Our acne facials deep-clean pores, clear blackheads, rebalance oil and calm inflammation without stripping your skin.

Book a skin analysis Recommended treatments

What is it?

Breakouts happen when pores become clogged with excess sebum and dead skin cells, allowing acne-causing bacteria to multiply and trigger inflammation. They range from blackheads and whiteheads to red, inflamed papules.

What causes it?

  • Excess sebum production (oily and combination skin)
  • Dead skin cells that are not exfoliated away
  • Bacteria and inflammation inside the pore
  • Hormonal changes, stress and travel
  • Heavy, pore-clogging sunscreens and products

Why Bali makes it worse

Heat and humidity increase sweat and oil production, and thick sunscreen layered over sweat can block pores. Changes in diet, water, sleep and routine when you travel add to it. Many visitors notice their skin breaking out within the first week on the island.

Our approach at POINT · FACE

We clean the pore in depth without aggressive scrubbing: double cleansing, AHA/BHA exfoliation, Hydrafacial vortex extraction and careful manual extraction only where needed. Then we calm and protect with niacinamide, tea tree, centella, high frequency and blue & red LED.

At home

Daily care in Bali

  • Choose a light, non-comedogenic SPF 50 (gel or fluid textures)
  • Double cleanse in the evening to remove sunscreen and sweat
  • Rinse your face with fresh water after the sea or pool
  • Do not pick — it causes marks that last longer than the spot

FAQ

Acne, Breakouts & Blackheads: your questions

How many acne facials do I need?

For active acne we recommend 3 sessions spaced 2–3 weeks apart, then one session every 2 months as maintenance.

Do you do extractions?

Yes, gently and only where needed, after the pores have been softened. The Hydrafacial vortex removes most congestion without squeezing.

Can you treat acne marks?

Post-acne marks (dark spots) respond well to our Bright & Pigment Correct facial once breakouts are under control.
