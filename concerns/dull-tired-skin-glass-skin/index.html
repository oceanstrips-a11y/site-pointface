Glass Skin Facial in Uluwatu, Bali — For Dull, Tired Skin

Glass skin is the Korean ideal of skin so smooth, hydrated and clear it reflects light like glass. It is not about make-up — it is about hydration, texture and a healthy barrier.

Book a skin analysis Recommended treatments

What is it?

Dull skin has lost its radiance: dead cells accumulate on the surface, the skin is dehydrated and light no longer reflects evenly. Glass skin is the opposite — clear, plump, refined and luminous.

What causes it?

  • A build-up of dead skin cells
  • Dehydration and a weakened barrier
  • Fatigue, jet lag, stress and late nights
  • Congested pores and uneven texture

Why Bali makes it worse

Holiday rhythm — flights, sun, late dinners and sunset drinks — shows on the skin. A single glass-skin facial at the start or end of your trip resets texture and radiance.

Our 18+ step Korean protocol exfoliates, decongests, hydrates in depth and seals: hydrodermabrasion, peptide and snail-mucin infusion, oxygen, high frequency, cold hammer and red & green LED.
