In short

A purifying Korean treatment for chest skin prone to breakouts, clogged pores and excess sebum. Recommended for: Congested, oily or blemish-prone chest skin.

Targeted Hydrafacial cleansing, gentle exfoliation and calming Korean skincare remove surface impurities, refine uneven texture and improve the appearance of blemishes. Enriched with SKIN1004 Tea-Trica for a clearer, fresher, more balanced décolleté.

Key ingredients

  • Tea Tree (Tea-Trica)
  • Centella Asiatica
  • Niacinamide
  • Korean botanical extracts
  • Purifying mask

Key technologies

  • Hydrodermabrasion
  • Ultrasonic infusion
  • LED light therapy (red & green)
  • Cold Hammer
  • Relaxing massage

Results

  • Clearer, smoother skin
  • Reduced breakouts & blemishes
  • Less clogged pores
  • Balanced sebum production
  • Calmer, healthier-looking chest skin

For best results

A series of 3 sessions spaced 2–3 weeks apart is recommended, followed by regular maintenance.

Ideal for

Acne, Breakouts & Blackheads

Aftercare

  • Avoid touching or picking the treated area
  • No sun exposure for 24–48 h — SPF 50 is essential
  • Avoid sweating, sauna & intense workouts (and surfing) for 24 h
  • Use only gentle, hydrating skincare
  • No acids, retinol or harsh products for 5–7 days
