The short answer

The best Hydrafacial in Bali starts with a real skin analysis, uses a multi-function Hydrafacial system (hydrodermabrasion, extraction, ultrasonic and oxygen infusion, radiofrequency, LED), infuses actives chosen for your concern, and ends with aftercare adapted to sun and sea. In Uluwatu, POINT · FACE offers five Korean Hydrafacials at both studios, three HydroJelly Hydrafacials in Ungasan and Korean Body Hydrafacials in Bingin.

Key takeaways

  • Look for a skin analysis before the treatment.
  • Check the technologies and the skincare brands used.
  • The protocol should match your concern, not be one-size-fits-all.
  • Clear prices, tax and service included, and aftercare advice.

1. A skin analysis first

A great Hydrafacial is tailored. At POINT · FACE every treatment begins with a free AI-powered skin analysis of hydration, oil, pores, pigmentation and sensitivity.

2. Real technology, not just a water peel

Hydrodermabrasion is the base, but the best results come from combining steps. Our systems cleanse (hydrodermabrasion, ultrasonic scrubber, vacuum and spot extraction), infuse (oxygen, ultrasound, eye ultrasound), lift (radiofrequency, microcurrent, multipolar lifting) and calm (high frequency, cryo, LED). See the full technology table.

3. Actives chosen for your concern

  • Glow & hydration: Ultimate Korean Glass Skin & Hydralift (our best seller, 18+ steps)
  • Firmness: Advanced Collagen Booster
  • Repair & sensitivity: PDRN Cellular Repair
  • Acne & congestion: K-Balance Acne Clear
  • Radiance & even tone: Vitamin C Sunshine Glow

4. The HydroJelly finish (Ungasan)

Our Caviar Luxury Anti-Aging & Lift, Cryo Skin Reset and Bright & Pigment Correct end with a HydroJelly mask that seals in actives. They are exclusive to our Uluwatu Ungasan studio.

5. Beyond the face (Bingin)

Back breakouts, chest pigmentation, bumps on the booty: our Korean Body Hydrafacials bring the same technology to the body, exclusively at Uluwatu Bingin.

6. Transparent prices and packages

All prices include the 15% tax and service charge. For lasting results, our 3-session packages save up to 30%.

FAQ

How long does a Hydrafacial last?

The glow is visible immediately and lasts about a week. For lasting change, plan a series of 3 sessions spaced 2–3 weeks apart.

Is a Hydrafacial painful?

No. Most people find it relaxing; the suction feels cool and slightly ticklish.
